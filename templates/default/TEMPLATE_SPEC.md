# Meeting Minutes Template Specification

This document describes the structure and formatting conventions for meeting
minutes generated by MinuteMaker. It is the authoritative reference for how
raw notes should be categorised into sections.

## Sections (in order)

1. **Welcome & Attendance** — who was present, apologies, quorum
2. **Updates** — standing items, progress reports, follow-ups from previous meetings
3. **Discussion Items** — new topics raised and discussed during the meeting
4. **Action Items** — tasks assigned, with responsible person and deadline if stated
5. **Any Other Business** — anything not covered by the sections above
6. **Next Meeting** — date, time, location, chair/secretary for the next meeting

## Content Guidelines

- Each section's content is a list of bullet points.
- If a section has nothing to report, say "Nothing to report."
- Write in third person, past tense.
- Be concise but complete — each bullet should be self-contained.
- Use `\textbf{}` for deadlines and action items.
- For links, use `\href{URL}{descriptive text}` — not raw URLs.

## Chair and Secretary

If the raw notes explicitly state who chaired or minuted the meeting, include
their names. Otherwise use "Check Meeting Schedule" as a placeholder.

## Next Meeting Section

This section should always include:
- Date (if stated)
- Chair (if stated)
- Secretary (if stated)
